Determinan Matriks#

Halaman ini membahas cara mencari determinan matriks.

Mencari Determinan 2×2#

Diketahui:

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 1 & 4 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Rumus Determinan 2×2#

\(\det(A)\)

\(= (a \times d) - (b \times c)\)

Identifikasi Elemen#

\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

=

\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Maka:

\(a = 3\) \(b = 2\) \(c = 1\) \(d = 4\)

Substitusi ke Rumus#

\(\det(A)\)

\[ = (3 \times 4) - (2 \times 1) \]
\[ = 12 - 2 \]
\[ = 10 \]

Jawaban Akhir#

\(\det(A)\) = 10

Bentuk Ekspansi Baris 1#

Baris pertama:

\[ [3 \quad 2] \]

Pola tanda:

Maka:

\(\det(A)\)

\[ = 3(4) - 2(1) \]
\[ = 12 - 2 \]
\[ = 10 \]

Kesimpulan#

Untuk matriks 2×2:

\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

\(\det(A)\)

\(= a(d) - b(c)\)

\(= ad - bc\)

Mencari Determinan 3×3 dengan Ekspansi Baris Pertama#

Diketahui:

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ 1 & 0 & 6 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Langkah 1: Pilih Baris Pertama#

Baris pertama:

\[ [1 \quad 2 \quad 3] \]

Pola tanda:

Maka:

\(\det(A)\)

= 1(\(M_{11}\)) - 2(\(M_{12}\)) + 3(\(M_{13}\))

Langkah 2: Cari Minor \(M_{11}\)#

Coret baris 1 dan kolom 1

\(M_{11}\) =

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ 0 & 6 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

\(\det(M_{11})\)

\[ = (4\times6) - (5\times0) \]
\[ = 24 \]

Langkah 3: Cari Minor \(M_{12}\)#

Coret baris 1 dan kolom 2

\(M_{12}\) =

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 5 \\ 1 & 6 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

\(\det(M_{12})\)

\[ = (0\times6) - (5\times1) \]
\[ = -5 \]

Langkah 4: Cari Minor \(M_{13}\)#

Coret baris 1 dan kolom 3

\(M_{13}\) =

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 4 \\ 1 & 0 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

\(\det(M_{13})\)

\[ = (0\times0) - (4\times1) \]
\[ = -4 \]

Langkah 5: Substitusi ke Rumus#

\(\det(A)\)

\[ = 1(24) - 2(-5) + 3(-4) \]
\[ = 24 + 10 - 12 \]
\[ = 22 \]

Jawaban Akhir#

\(\det(A)\) = 22

Pola yang Harus Diingat#

Untuk ekspansi baris pertama matriks 3×3:

\(\det(A)\)

= a(\(M_{11}\)) - b(\(M_{12}\)) + c(\(M_{13}\))

Pola tanda:

Langkah:

  1. Pilih baris.

  2. Coret baris dan kolom elemen tersebut.

  3. Hitung minor 2×2.

  4. Kalikan dengan tanda (+ - +).

  5. Jumlahkan semuanya.

Mencari Determinan 4×4 dengan Ekspansi Baris Pertama#

Diketahui:

|A| =

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 & 4 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Langkah 1: Pilih Baris Pertama#

Baris pertama:

\[ [2 \quad 0 \quad 1 \quad 0] \]

Pola tanda:

Maka:

\(\det(A)\)

\[ = 2M_{11} - 0M_{12} + 1M_{13} - 0M_{14} \]

Karena ada nol:

= 2M11 + \(M_{13}\)

Langkah 2: Cari Minor \(M_{11}\)#

Coret baris 1 dan kolom 1

\(M_{11}\) =

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 4 \\ 2 & 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (1 \times 3 \times 3) + (2 \times 1 \times 1) + (4 \times 2 \times 2) \]
\[ = 9 + 2 + 16 \]
\[ = 27 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (1 \times 3 \times 4) + (2 \times 1 \times 1) + (3 \times 2 \times 2) \]
\[ = 12 + 2 + 12 \]
\[ = 26 \]

\(M_{11}\) = 27 - 26

\(M_{11}\) = 1

Langkah 3: Cari Minor \(M_{13}\)#

Coret baris 1 dan kolom 3

\(M_{13}\) =

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 1 & 4 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (3 \times 2 \times 3) + (1 \times 1 \times 0) + (4 \times 1 \times 1) \]
\[ = 18 + 0 + 4 \]
\[ = 22 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (0 \times 2 \times 4) + (1 \times 1 \times 3) + (3 \times 1 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 3 + 3 \]
\[ = 6 \]

\(M_{13}\) = 22 - 6

\(M_{13}\) = 16

Langkah 4: Substitusi ke Rumus#

\(\det(A)\)

= 2(\(M_{11}\)) + \(M_{13}\)

\[ = 2(1) + 16 \]
\[ = 2 + 16 \]
\[ = 18 \]

Jawaban Akhir#

\(\det(A)\) = 18

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 & 4 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

= -\(M_{21}\) - 2M23 + 4M24

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (1 \times 2 \times 3) + (0 \times 1 \times 2) + (3 \times 1 \times 1) \]
\[ = 6 + 0 + 3 \]
\[ = 9 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (2 \times 2 \times 3) + (1 \times 1 \times 1) + (3 \times 1 \times 0) \]
\[ = 12 + 1 + 0 \]
\[ = 13 \]

\(M_{21}\)

\[ = 9 - 13 \]
\[ = -4 \]

Langkah 3: Cari Minor \(M_{23}\)#

Coret baris 2 dan kolom 3

\(M_{23}\) =

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 3 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (2 \times 1 \times 3) + (1 \times 1 \times 0) + (3 \times 3 \times 2) \]
\[ = 6 + 0 + 18 \]
\[ = 24 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (0 \times 1 \times 3) + (2 \times 1 \times 2) + (3 \times 3 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 4 + 9 \]
\[ = 13 \]

\(M_{23}\)

\[ = 24 - 13 \]
\[ = 11 \]

Langkah 4: Cari Minor \(M_{24}\)#

Coret baris 2 dan kolom 4

\(M_{24}\) =

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (2 \times 1 \times 1) + (1 \times 2 \times 0) + (0 \times 3 \times 2) \]
\[ = 2 + 0 + 0 \]
\[ = 2 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (0 \times 1 \times 0) + (2 \times 2 \times 2) + (1 \times 3 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 8 + 3 \]
\[ = 11 \]

\(M_{24}\)

\[ = 2 - 11 \]
\[ = -9 \]

Langkah 5: Substitusi#

\(\det(A)\)

\[ = -(-4) - 2(11) + 4(-9) \]
\[ = 4 - 22 - 36 \]
\[ = -54 \]

Jawaban Akhir#

\(\det(A)\) = -54

Mencari Determinan 4×4 dengan Ekspansi Kolom Kedua#

\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Pola tanda kolom kedua:

Maka:

\(\det(A)\)

= -1(\(M_{12}\)) +0(\(M_{22}\)) -1(\(M_{32}\)) +2(\(M_{42}\))

Karena ada nol:

\(\det(A)\)

= -\(M_{12}\) - \(M_{32}\) + 2M42

Langkah 2: Cari Minor \(M_{12}\)#

Coret baris 1 dan kolom 2

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 4 \\ 3 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (1 \times 2 \times 3) + (2 \times 1 \times 0) + (4 \times 3 \times 1) \]
\[ = 6 + 0 + 12 \]
\[ = 18 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (0 \times 2 \times 4) + (1 \times 1 \times 1) + (3 \times 3 \times 2) \]
\[ = 0 + 1 + 18 \]
\[ = 19 \]

\(M_{12}\)

\[ = 18 - 19 \]
\[ = -1 \]

Langkah 3: Cari Minor \(M_{32}\)#

Coret baris 3 dan kolom 2

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 & 3 \\ 1 & 2 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (2 \times 2 \times 3) + (0 \times 4 \times 0) + (3 \times 1 \times 1) \]
\[ = 12 + 0 + 3 \]
\[ = 15 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (0 \times 2 \times 3) + (1 \times 4 \times 2) + (3 \times 1 \times 0) \]
\[ = 0 + 8 + 0 \]
\[ = 8 \]

\(M_{32}\)

\[ = 15 - 8 \]
\[ = 7 \]

Langkah 4: Cari Minor \(M_{42}\)#

Coret baris 4 dan kolom 2

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 & 3 \\ 1 & 2 & 4 \\ 3 & 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (2 \times 2 \times 1) + (0 \times 4 \times 3) + (3 \times 1 \times 2) \]
\[ = 4 + 0 + 6 \]
\[ = 10 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (3 \times 2 \times 3) + (2 \times 4 \times 2) + (1 \times 1 \times 0) \]
\[ = 18 + 16 + 0 \]
\[ = 34 \]

\(M_{42}\)

\[ = 10 - 34 \]
\[ = -24 \]

Langkah 5: Substitusi#

\(\det(A)\)

\[ = -(-1) - (7) + 2(-24) \]
\[ = 1 - 7 - 48 \]
\[ = -54 \]

Jawaban Akhir#

\(\det(A)\) = -54

Pola Tanda Kolom Kedua#

Posisi:

(1,2) = -

(2,2) = +

(3,2) = -

(4,2) = +

Sehingga:

Semua ekspansi (baris atau kolom mana pun) harus menghasilkan nilai determinan yang sama.

Mencari Determinan 4×4 dengan Ekspansi Kolom Ketiga#

Diketahui:

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 & 4 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Langkah 1: Pilih Kolom Ketiga#

Kolom ketiga:

\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 2 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Pola tanda kolom ketiga:

Maka:

\(\det(A)\)

= 0(\(M_{13}\)) -2(\(M_{23}\)) +2(\(M_{33}\)) -1(\(M_{43}\))

Karena ada nol:

\(\det(A)\)

= -2(\(M_{23}\)) +2(\(M_{33}\)) -\(M_{43}\)

Langkah 2: Cari Minor \(M_{23}\)#

Coret baris 2 dan kolom 3

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 3 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (2 \times 1 \times 3) + (1 \times 1 \times 0) + (3 \times 3 \times 2) \]
\[ = 6 + 0 + 18 \]
\[ = 24 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (0 \times 1 \times 3) + (2 \times 1 \times 2) + (3 \times 3 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 4 + 9 \]
\[ = 13 \]

\(M_{23}\)

\[ = 24 - 13 \]
\[ = 11 \]

Langkah 3: Cari Minor \(M_{33}\)#

Coret baris 3 dan kolom 3

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 & 4 \\ 0 & 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (2 \times 0 \times 3) + (1 \times 4 \times 0) + (3 \times 1 \times 2) \]
\[ = 0 + 0 + 6 \]
\[ = 6 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (0 \times 0 \times 3) + (2 \times 4 \times 2) + (3 \times 1 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 16 + 3 \]
\[ = 19 \]

\(M_{33}\)

\[ = 6 - 19 \]
\[ = -13 \]

Langkah 4: Cari Minor \(M_{43}\)#

Coret baris 4 dan kolom 3

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 & 4 \\ 3 & 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (2 \times 0 \times 1) + (1 \times 4 \times 3) + (3 \times 1 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 12 + 3 \]
\[ = 15 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (3 \times 0 \times 3) + (1 \times 4 \times 2) + (1 \times 1 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 8 + 1 \]
\[ = 9 \]

\(M_{43}\)

\[ = 15 - 9 \]
\[ = 6 \]

Langkah 5: Substitusi#

\(\det(A)\)

\[ = -2(11) \]

+2(-13) -6

\[ = -22 - 26 - 6 \]
\[ = -54 \]

Jawaban Akhir#

\(\det(A)\) = -54

Pola Tanda Kolom Ketiga#

Posisi:

(1,3) = +

(2,3) = -

(3,3) = +

(4,3) = -

Sehingga:

Semua ekspansi baris maupun kolom harus menghasilkan determinan yang sama.

Mencari Determinan 4×4 dengan Ekspansi Kolom Keempat#

Diketahui:

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 & 4 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Langkah 1: Pilih Kolom Keempat#

Kolom keempat:

\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \\ 1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Pola tanda kolom keempat:

Maka:

\(\det(A)\)

= -3(\(M_{14}\)) +4(\(M_{24}\)) -1(\(M_{34}\)) +3(\(M_{44}\))

Langkah 2: Cari Minor \(M_{14}\)#

Coret baris 1 dan kolom 4

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (1 \times 1 \times 1) + (0 \times 2 \times 0) + (2 \times 3 \times 2) \]
\[ = 1 + 0 + 12 \]
\[ = 13 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (0 \times 1 \times 2) + (2 \times 2 \times 1) + (1 \times 3 \times 0) \]
\[ = 0 + 4 + 0 \]
\[ = 4 \]

\(M_{14}\)

\[ = 13 - 4 \]
\[ = 9 \]

Langkah 3: Cari Minor \(M_{24}\)#

Coret baris 2 dan kolom 4

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (2 \times 1 \times 1) + (1 \times 2 \times 0) + (0 \times 3 \times 2) \]
\[ = 2 + 0 + 0 \]
\[ = 2 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (0 \times 1 \times 0) + (2 \times 2 \times 2) + (1 \times 3 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 8 + 3 \]
\[ = 11 \]

\(M_{24}\)

\[ = 2 - 11 \]
\[ = -9 \]

Langkah 4: Cari Minor \(M_{34}\)#

Coret baris 3 dan kolom 4

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (2 \times 0 \times 1) + (1 \times 2 \times 0) + (0 \times 1 \times 2) \]
\[ = 0 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (0 \times 0 \times 0) + (2 \times 2 \times 2) + (1 \times 1 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 8 + 1 \]
\[ = 9 \]

\(M_{34}\)

\[ = 0 - 9 \]
\[ = -9 \]

Langkah 5: Cari Minor \(M_{44}\)#

Coret baris 4 dan kolom 4

\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hitung dengan Sarrus:

Diagonal turun:

\[ (2 \times 0 \times 2) + (1 \times 2 \times 3) + (0 \times 1 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 6 + 0 \]
\[ = 6 \]

Diagonal naik:

\[ (3 \times 0 \times 0) + (1 \times 2 \times 2) + (2 \times 1 \times 1) \]
\[ = 0 + 4 + 2 \]
\[ = 6 \]

\(M_{44}\)

\[ = 6 - 6 \]
\[ = 0 \]

Langkah 6: Substitusi#

\(\det(A)\)

\[ = -3(9) \]

+4(-9) -1(-9) +3(0)

\[ = -27 - 36 + 9 + 0 \]
\[ = -54 \]

Jawaban Akhir#

\(\det(A)\) = -54

Pola Tanda Kolom Keempat#

Posisi:

(1,4) = -

(2,4) = +

(3,4) = -

(4,4) = +

Sehingga:

Kesimpulan#

Untuk matriks 4×4 ini:

  • Ekspansi Baris 1 = -54

  • Ekspansi Baris 2 = -54

  • Ekspansi Baris 3 = -54

  • Ekspansi Baris 4 = -54

  • Ekspansi Kolom 1 = -54

  • Ekspansi Kolom 2 = -54

  • Ekspansi Kolom 3 = -54

  • Ekspansi Kolom 4 = -54

Semua menghasilkan nilai determinan yang sama:

\(\det(A)\) = -54
